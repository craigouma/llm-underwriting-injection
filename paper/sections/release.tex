\section{Responsible release}

All data and payloads are synthetic. The documents are generated from rows of a public credit dataset with fictional employers and identifiers. No real applicant, company or system was used or attacked. The injection payloads are generic strings that already appear in the prompt-injection literature, and they are released so that others can reproduce the evaluation and test their own defenses. The falsification variants describe ordinary application fraud, and the study reports where defenses against it fail. The scorecard is a research model trained on a 2011 competition dataset. It has not been validated for lending and has had no fairness analysis, and it should not be used on real applicants. This is independent research and has not been reviewed or endorsed by any lender, model provider or Kaggle.
