\begin{abstract}
\noindent Lenders have started to let LLM agents read applicant documents, and applicants control those documents. We ask where the LLM should sit in an automated credit underwriting pipeline. On synthetic payslip, bank statement and application-form bundles generated from \nCleanRows{} real credit records, we compare four configurations under \nAttacks{} attack variants and three open-weight models of \modelRange{}: an LLM that decides, the same with a prompt-level defense, an LLM that only extracts fields for a logistic scorecard (B), and B with schema, cross-document and plausible-band checks and a human-review rule (C). For the two models that do not approve everyone, injected instructions steer the deciding LLM in \asrPhiAInstr{}\% and \asrOlmoAInstr{}\% of attacked cases and still in \asrPhiAdefInstr{}\% and \asrOlmoAdefInstr{}\% with a prompt defense. As extractors the same models are steered in \asrPhiBInstr{}\% and \asrOlmoBInstr{}\% of cases, mostly by payloads that name the values to write, and C records no success in \asrPhiCInstrn{} Phi pairs or \asrOlmoCInstrn{} Olmo pairs (95\% upper bounds \asrPhiCInstrhi{}\% and \asrOlmoCInstrhi{}\%). Falsified documents succeed against B in \asrPhiBExtract{}\% to \asrQwenBExtract{}\% of cases, about as often as or less often than with an oracle extractor that makes no error, so the LLM adds no success of its own. C lowers it to \asrQwenCExtract{}\% to \asrOlmoCExtract{}\% and flags \reviewPhiC{}\% to \reviewQwenC{}\% of clean bundles for review, a cost that follows mainly from extraction errors, since \flagWrongPhi{}\% to \flagWrongQwen{}\% of the flagged bundles contain a wrongly extracted or missing field. Olmo-3 7B ran on 200 applicants and 50 targets, so its intervals are wide. Per model, the preregistered rules are met as follows: H1 (B at or below 2\% on instruction attacks) for one of three models, H2 (extraction-manipulation success rising with plausibility) for two, and H3 (C lowers extraction success at a higher review rate) for all three. By the majority rule H1 is not supported and H2 and H3 are.
\end{abstract}
